# Synthetic Data Card

## Identity

- **Dataset:** Synthetic Retail Order Fulfillment Dataset
- **Generator:** `src/generate_synthetic_dataset.py`
- **Seed:** `20261023` (deterministic using Python’s standard library)
- **Coverage:** 2025-01-01 through 2026-06-30
- **Markets:** US and IN, with local currency values in USD and INR
- **Current size:** 1,440 orders, 3,601 order lines, 80 customers, 24 products, and 6 carriers

## Intended use

Use this dataset to demonstrate reproducible data generation, ETL design, schema mapping, SQL joins and aggregation, data-quality checks, workflow automation, dashboard mechanics, and AI-output validation. It is suitable for a technical portfolio walkthrough when its synthetic nature is prominent and unambiguous.

## Construction and design

Every record and name is generated by code; no company’s or person’s private data is used. The generator deliberately varies delivery punctuality across fictional carriers and markets and partial fulfillment across fictional markets and product categories. It also creates a small number of cancelled orders. These programmed differences make segmentation queries and tests possible. They are not estimates of real-world rates or industry benchmarks.

Separate validation fixtures deliberately include malformed or referentially invalid records so that an ETL workflow can demonstrate error handling. Fixtures are not part of the analytical dataset.

## Limitations and prohibited interpretation

- No real operations, customers, supplier relationships, carriers, or transactions are represented.
- A calculated metric describes only these generated rows and reflects the generator’s rules.
- A segment ranking cannot establish a real causal driver or operational problem.
- Currency prices are synthetic local values; USD and INR must not be combined without a separate conversion basis.
- The dataset contains no on-hand inventory, stock movements, purchase orders, supplier lead times, actual carrier event history, costs of failure, or real financial impact.
- Do not claim actual company improvement, savings, service performance, stockout causes, safety stock, or reorder points from these files.

## Recommended portfolio disclosure

> “I built and analyzed a deterministic synthetic dataset to demonstrate an order-fulfillment ETL and analytics workflow. The records and programmed patterns are fictional; metrics are used to demonstrate calculation and validation, not to describe a real business.”

## Reproducibility

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 src/generate_synthetic_dataset.py
```

The generator overwrites the files under `data/synthetic/` and `data/fixtures/`. To preserve reproducibility, commit the generator and its fixed seed with the data files. If the generator logic or seed changes, update this card and review dependent documentation.
